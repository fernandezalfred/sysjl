## Status

In Progress

## Goals

- In Sale Records (`/dashboard`) and Totals by User (`/`), the "Ganador" / "Ganador (+Bs …)" label moves from the Total Revenue cell to under the Milk & Mote amount (desktop and mobile).
- Same visibility as before: margin only for admins.
- The seller pay line is labeled "Pago" / "Pay" instead of "Pago del vendedor" / "Seller pay" in both Sale Records and Totals by User (`cell.pay` replaces `cell.sellerPay`).
- "Total vendidos más no vendidos" in both tables' total cell is shown as the sum itself, e.g. "2.830,00 + 726,00 = Bs 3.556,00": Total venta + No vendidos in muted text, the result bold in amber (`text-amber-700` / dark `text-amber-400`); the label text is removed from view, kept as `title` tooltip and `aria-label`. New `formatAmount` (number without "Bs") in `src/lib/pricing.ts`, unit-tested.
- "Ganancia neta" in both tables' total cell: the amount is bold, larger and purple (`text-purple-700` / dark `text-purple-400`), chosen by the user over green (confused with the winner) after a solid emerald/red pill was rejected (`NetProfitLine` component; `LabeledAmount` gained `amountClassName`). The day header's net profit is unchanged.
- Every "Editar" / "Edit" link (Sale Records, Totals by User, Milk, Grains; desktop and mobile, 8 in all) is now one shared `EditButton` (`src/components/ui/EditButton.tsx`): just a pencil icon, no border, background or text (the user preferred it over an outlined button with text); turns blue on hover with a pointer cursor; the label is the `title` tooltip and `aria-label`; `-m-2 p-2` keeps a larger tap area.
- (Separate fix, own commit) When a form's server validation fails, the fields keep what the user typed instead of being cleared.
- The user name (only the name text) shows in blue (`text-sky-700` / dark `text-sky-400`) and uppercase in both tables.
- More visible, uppercase table headers in both tables: inverted header row (`bg-foreground text-background`, semibold, `text-xs uppercase tracking-wide`); on mobile, the Queso / Yogurt / Leche y Mote / Ingreso total labels are uppercase, semibold and darker.
- Each day header in both tables shows that day's total Net profit on the right ("Ganancia neta: Bs …"), admins only, counted over every record of the day (not just the current page).
- "Pago del vendedor" moves from the Total Revenue cell to under the seller's name in both tables (desktop and mobile); same visibility (admins every row, a seller their own rows, guests none).
